# AI readiness grading rubric

Grade only from the evidence you are given. Do not research and do not add facts.

## Scale

5 Prime · 4 Strong · 3 Qualified · 2 Emerging · 1 Early · 0 None

## Descriptors per dimension

| Dimension | 5 Prime | 3 Qualified | 1 Early |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use Case Readiness | Several evidenced, quantified pains with an owner and budget | Concrete pains are visible, not yet quantified or owned | No identifiable pain or use case |
| Organisational | AI in stated strategy, executive sponsor, budget, governance | Digital or data strategy exists; AI mentioned but not owned | No digital, data or AI direction visible |
| Team & Capability | Dedicated data or AI team in place and delivering | Some data or IT staff, or a reliable provider; no dedicated AI skills | No dedicated data or AI capability; IT covered minimally or outsourced |
| Data | Data centralised, governed and accessible for use cases | Relevant data exists in systems but is siloed or hard to reach | Little digitised or accessible data |
| Tech Stack | Modern core systems, integration layer, data platform | Core systems known (ERP, CRM); limited integration | Stack unknown or legacy with no integration |
| Infrastructure | Cloud or hosting strategy defined, ready for AI workloads, sovereignty addressed | Some cloud use; no AI-ready platform | No disclosed cloud or hosting posture |
| Regulatory | Applicable rules mapped, compliance function engaged on AI | Strong compliance culture; AI-specific obligations not yet addressed | Obligations unknown or unaddressed |

Grades 4 and 2 sit between the descriptors on either side.

## Method: do this for every dimension, in order

1. **Evidence**: list the facts from the research that bear on it.
2. **Gaps**: list what is missing.
3. **Criterion**: state which descriptor the evidence meets.
4. **Grade**: only then give the number.

## Rules

- Missing evidence caps the grade at 2 and the tag is Inferred. Say what is missing.
- Each assessment is 2 to 4 full sentences: the evidence, the numbers, what it means for an engagement.
- Tag each assessment with the weakest evidence it relies on: Confirmed, Research or Inferred.
- Precise, never punchy. No metaphors. Write "no dedicated data or AI team", never "no team": someone always covers IT.
- A low grade is a finding, not a criticism. Phrase it as the support the client will need.

## Output

Table: Dimension | Grade | Tag | Assessment, in the order of the rubric. Then the Working section (steps 1 to 3 per dimension) at the end.
